Data and assumptions

Monthly means are weighted by the number of calendar days. All 12 valid months are required for an annual mean. Missing values are not filled. Trends use ordinary least squares on complete annual means; confidence intervals use Newey-West covariance and approximate Student-t inference.

This is an exploratory estimate, not a forecast or causal attribution. Short records, missing years, and persistent autocorrelation can affect inference. Source identity is user-supplied: synthetic demo inputs are not NASA observations.
